Fig. 1. Overview of SYMBILIST. (a) Shared scored N-best lists are processed through two anchor views, listwise ranking, and anti-symmetric arbitration. Agreement boundaries require unanimity for two sources and a two-of-three quorum for three sources, with dissenting tokens remaining selectable in the latter case. CRC deferral and CPU/GPU routing are optional. (b) A Hausa example from FLEURS development data illustrates intact phrase recombination and contrasts fixed-length and agreement-anchored partitioning. Check marks and crosses indicate reference-based local matches and errors. Error counts cover the full utterance.
